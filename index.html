Trust & Anti-Fraud

From Green Action to Verified Point

No self-reporting. No honor system. Every reward is machine-verified before it's credited.

1. Passenger Action

Selects "Metro commute", "Light bag" or "Meal opt-out" in-app before check-in.

2. Airline / Airport Verification

GPS/geofence at transit gates, NFC taps, or the check-in counter's digital scale.

3. FLYNT API

Matches the sensor event to the passenger's PNR, flight window, and fraud filter.

4. Green Points Awarded

Wallet updates instantly; an auditable log is kept for airline and sponsor.

Hardware & API Scale Link

Integrated with airport luggage scales & gate NFC readers for zero-friction verification.

PNR & Fraud Ledger

Automated passenger record check eliminates double-claiming or false location tags.

Instant Point Settlement

Sponsors get real-time audit logs for every single point issued on the platform.

Sponsor Economics

Why a Brand Funds Green Points

Sponsors aren't donating — they're buying reach, acquisition, and an ESG story with data behind it.

Brand exposure

Direct visibility to frequent flyers and business travelers inside the lounge and app.

Customer acquisition

Every voucher claim introduces a high-income traveler to the sponsor's product.

ESG marketing

Funds a behavior-change program the brand can report in its own sustainability disclosures.

Customer engagement

Pay for verified redemptions, not impressions — every claim is a logged, real action.

Airport advertising, reimagined

The VR Corner and lounge placement give sponsors a tangible presence, not a static banner.

Lower cost than media buys

Funding a voucher a traveler actually redeems typically beats comparable airport media rates.
